FAQ - Example Product

Q: What is the product's warranty period?
A: 18 months from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects.

Q: How do I request a refund?
A: Refunds can be requested within 7 calendar days of receiving the product,
through the customer portal, under the "My Orders" section.

Q: Does the product work offline?
A: Yes, all core features work without an internet connection, except for
cloud data synchronization.
